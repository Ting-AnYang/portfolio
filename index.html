ABOUT ME
▶ TING-AN YANG
Ting-An Yang is a Taiwanese artist specializing in illustration. She is currently studying Art and Art History at New York University Abu Dhabi.

Starting out as self-taught artist, she now works in both traditional and digital mediums, recently venturing into traditional printmaking and sculpting.

She is firmly against the use of any generative AI in art.
